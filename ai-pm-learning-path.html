Learning tracks

Track A Ground the Fundamentals

In order. Closes the "can defend the internals" gap. Prioritised for frontier-lab interviews.

Hugging Face — LLM Course
Covers training, RLHF, fine-tuning, deployment, inference economics properly. Best single resource for understanding why what you've been running behaves as it does.
Free Cert on completion
Karpathy — "Intro to LLMs" + "Let's build GPT"
YouTube. After HF course. Cements attention, tokenization, and scaling mechanics so you stop taking them on faith. Essential before any Anthropic conversation.
Free
Chip Huyen — AI Engineering (2025)
Production-stack bible. Eval design, RAG tradeoffs, fine-tuning vs. prompting decisions, inference cost models. Lets you argue tradeoffs confidently — the exact thing frontier-lab PMs are tested on.
~₹2,500 book
Anthropic-Specific Reading
Know these cold before any Anthropic conversation:

Claude's Model Spec — capability vs. safety tradeoff thinking
Constitutional AI paper (Bai et al., 2022) — how Claude is trained
Scaling Laws (Kaplan et al.) — how labs make investment decisions
Interpretability blog posts — what they're solving and why it shapes product
Free Anthropic-specific
Track B Credentials Worth Getting

One platform cert + one skills cert. Don't collect all of them — signals breadth without depth.

Hugging Face Certification
Earned by completing the LLM course. Most authentic practitioner signal available — proves hands-on LLM competence, not vendor familiarity. Reads better than AWS at frontier labs.
Free (via course) Cert
AWS Certified AI Practitioner
~$100, 2–3 weeks. ATS-friendly, globally recognized. Clears HR filters. Not a differentiator at frontier labs — but it doesn't hurt and requires minimal prep given your background.
~$100 Cert
Google Cloud — Generative AI Learning Path
Refreshed for Gemini 2 + ADK. Worth it if Cohere or Vertex-adjacent roles are active. Skip if Anthropic/OpenAI are the only real targets — limited marginal signal there.
Free (audit) Cert
IBM AI PM Professional Certificate
Generic for the level you're targeting.
Skip
Track C Proof of Work

Your real differentiator. Empirical data at a scale most PM candidates don't have. Make it visible.

Evals Framework Write-up
What you built (Pydantic AI + pytest + Ragas + HTML dashboard), what broke, what you'd do differently. Frame as practitioner honesty, not expertise. Exactly the evidence Anthropic values.
Weeks 5–7
Telemetry Observations Post
1B+ tokens · 5 models · 17 languages · 11K+ requests. "Here's what I noticed about model behavior, cost curves, and failure modes at this scale." Present the data — don't overclaim.
Weeks 8–9
Product Teardown
Pick one target (Perplexity answer engine or a Shopify AI app). Apply Chip Huyen frameworks: likely eval strategy, RAG vs. fine-tuning tradeoffs, what you'd change. Shows applied synthesis.
Week 9

Free, 8 weeks, structured. Covers alignment, RLHF intent, Constitutional AI territory, and how safety tradeoffs shape product decisions. Most important addition for Anthropic specifically. No generic AI PM course covers this. Run in parallel with Anthropic reading from Week 1.
Free New Critical for Anthropic
Fast.ai — Practical Deep Learning Part 1 New
Free. Code-first, bottom-up. Highly respected by practitioners — fills the "can I actually read what's happening under the hood" gap that HF Course + Karpathy leaves if you don't code through them. Genuine technical credibility, not vocabulary.
Free New
DeepLearning.AI Short Courses New
1–2 hours each, most free. Prioritise: Building & Evaluating Advanced RAG, AI Agents in LangGraph, Evaluating & Debugging GenAI. Several co-produced with Anthropic or frontier-adjacent teams — direct signal.
